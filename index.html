01 / ABOUT

Academic excellence.
Engineering that matters.

Dr. Shaik Imamvali is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering at SR University, Warangal.

His academic work spans electromagnetic engineering, antenna design and optimization, computational modelling, biosensing and interdisciplinary research involving intelligent systems. His experience includes teaching, research, academic coordination, manuscript preparation, simulation and collaborative research.
